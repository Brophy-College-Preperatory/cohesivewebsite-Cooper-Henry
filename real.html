Skeletons: Real Life

Skeletons in real life are pretty interesting. Not going too deeply into the specifics, just the simple fact that a bunch of bones can define any shape of any being

It gets even more interesting when you realize that several plants, or other organisms without bones, often have substitues, like stems, that are ment to supplement bones

The fact that these couple of bones covered in nerves and muscles basically make animals specifics is incredible
